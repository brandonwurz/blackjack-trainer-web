The path: decisions first, money second

Seven levels — 0 through 6 — in the order the pros learn them. You can't count your way out of bad decisions, so basic strategy comes first — every level unlocks the next when you pass its test. Progress is saved on this device.

Count drills — Hi-Lo

Twos through sixes are +1, sevens through nines are 0, tens and aces are −1. Three drills: keep a running count of a dealt sequence at your chosen speed, count down a whole deck against the clock, and convert a running count to a true count for the number of decks in Rules.
